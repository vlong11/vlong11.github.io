Oil-Change Price Analysis

Examined oil-change prices across Chicago, Bolingbrook, and Peoria, comparing specialty-chain and general-service providers, including an interaction plot and two-way ANOVA.

Key findings

Location p = 0.287; service p = 0.804; interaction p = 0.854. None were significant at α = 0.05.
